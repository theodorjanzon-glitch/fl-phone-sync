Privacy policy

FL Phone Sync · Last updated 27 October 2026

What the app does

FL Phone Sync renders your FL Studio projects to audio on your computer and puts the audio files in a folder called “FL Phone Sync” in your own Google Drive, so you can play them on your phone.

What Google data it uses

The app asks for one Google permission: access to the Google Drive files it creates itself (the drive.file scope). It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive. It also reads your Google account’s email address to show which account you are signed in with.

Where your data goes

  • Your audio files go only to your own Google Drive. There is no FL Phone Sync server, and the developer never receives your files, your projects or your Google data.
  • Your sign-in stays on your own devices: on your computer in the app’s settings folder, and on your phone in the browser.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for ads.

FL Phone Sync’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Delete the “FL Phone Sync” folder in Google Drive to remove the tracks, and remove the app’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Signing out in the apps deletes the saved sign-in from that device.
